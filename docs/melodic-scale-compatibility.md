# Scale-guide compatibility

Full scales and modes must contain every written chord tone. This includes whole tone, augmented and diminished collections: they are offered for compatible chord types rather than admitted through generic tone omissions.

Pentatonic collections may omit chord tones if all their notes fit a seven-note guide that also contains every written chord tone. Blues collections use the same test for their underlying pentatonic; their added blue note is a passing colour. Full matches retain priority and all choices keep their existing family groups.

Examples: dominant 13 offers major pentatonic and major blues through Mixolydian. Minor 13 offers minor pentatonic and minor blues through Dorian. Minor 7 excludes Phrygian dominant. Plain dominant 7 excludes whole tone and altered from the guide menu; dominant 9 sharp 5 supports whole tone and an altered dominant supports altered. Minor blues over a major-third dominant is not automatically recommended by these conservative rules.

Accepted reduced collections do not trigger an incompatible-guide repair warning. Chord-tone exercises retain the written chord tones; scale exercises use the selected collection. Existing saved guides are preserved.

Validation: exhaustive chord/guide support assertions, all-key context checks, five/six-note pattern tests, production build and browser checks covering menus, warnings, mixed-chart playback and saved guides.
