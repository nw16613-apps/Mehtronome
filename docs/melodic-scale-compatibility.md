# Melodic scale compatibility

Scale guides no longer need to contain every written chord tone, regardless of collection size. Full chord matches retain priority; other usable collections appear in their existing family groups.

The filter rejects opposing major/minor thirds, opposing sevenths and conflicts with explicitly written altered fifths and extensions. Major/minor blues permit their characteristic passing inflections. Missing chord tones alone do not invalidate a guide. These are practical suggestions, not a claim that every scale note is equally stable over a chord.

Examples: dominant 13 offers major pentatonic, major blues and minor blues. Dominant 7 offers whole tone and altered as melodic colours. Minor 7 still excludes Phrygian dominant. Written flat-nine and sharp-five harmony still exclude conflicting natural-nine/fifth collections.

Accepted melodic colours no longer trigger the incompatible-guide repair warning. Chord-tone exercises retain the written chord tones; scale exercises use the selected collection. Existing saved guides are preserved.

Validation: focused scale, context and five/six-note pattern tests; production build; five browser checks covering menus, warnings, mixed-chart playback and saved guides.
