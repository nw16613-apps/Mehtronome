# Chord symbols and optional CAGED chord diagrams

Exercises print the initial backing chord and subsequent changes only. Repeated bars, continued systems and a CAGED-position change do not repeat an unchanged chord symbol. A change of chord quality or slash bass still prints. A later return to an earlier chord prints again. This applies to chord-aligned notation and the legacy standalone renderer/dynamic backing-symbol updater.

View → Chord diagrams is off initially. The checkbox is remembered in this browser and included in saved exercise options/history. When enabled, the first selected CAGED position and each subsequent CAGED-position change has a compact vertical chord diagram above the notation, with the position and framework label shown below the stave only. Low E is the left string. Dots are frets rather than prescribed finger numbers; × means mute and ○ means open. The adjacent fret number locates the shape on the neck.

The diagram calls the same recommended-grip selector as the linked CAGED fretboard, using that slot’s actual harmony and board. It does not substitute generic open chords or the starting chord’s shape. Adjacent CAGED regions can legitimately share a grip; they still get separate diagrams and below-stave position labels at a position change. If no suitable grip exists in that region, the header says “No grip in this position”. Extended harmony retains the existing grip selector’s omissions and the full backing chord symbol.

Diagrams live in the SVG header band. Measured bounds reserve space above notes, beams and triplet numbers, and diagrams render at 70% of their original size. Enabling diagrams keeps the same bars per system, score width and notation scale; only the required header height changes. Diagrams remain in TAB-only mode, exported SVG and printed/PDF notation. They do not affect exercise notes, timing, audio or CAGED assignments. Disabling the checkbox restores the regular score layout.

Unit checks cover actual chord pitches/fret coordinates in all twelve roots and five positions across minor, major, dominant, half-diminished and extended chords; position-change diagram placement with deduplicated symbols; unavailable-grip fallback; held chords across bars and systems; quality/slash-bass changes; and standalone symbol deduplication. Browser coverage checks the score against the linked CAGED grip, all subdivisions, measured music clearance, mobile width, SVG contents, toggle persistence and the off state. Existing harmonized-flow and positional-scale scenarios remain passing.

A chord change or fret-region shift within the same CAGED position does not add another diagram. Chord symbols continue to mark harmony changes independently; each diagram that is shown uses the harmony and grip at its position-change beat. Returning to an earlier position prints its diagram again.

The diagram itself has no visible position caption. The position remains in its accessible name. Below-stave position captions appear initially and at a CAGED-position change only, independently of whether diagrams are enabled, and do not repeat at subsequent bars or system breaks.
