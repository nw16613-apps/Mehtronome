# Written standard melodies

The Melodic movement track plays the supplied lead-sheet head when loading Autumn Leaves, All the Things You Are, There Will Never Be Another You or Blue Bossa from Harmony or Study Tier 4. Reload a standard from the menu to attach its melody to an older working chart. New saved charts and repertoire exports retain the melody identity.

Source: user-supplied lead-sheet screenshots, printed pages 39 (E minor), 22 (A-flat), 407 (E-flat) and 50 (C minor), respectively. Only the requested melody notes/rhythms are encoded; lyrics and unrelated pages are excluded. The separate introductions and codas are not part of the existing 32/36/32/16-bar charts. The Autumn Leaves first/second endings are unfolded into the 32-bar head. Ties are single sustained events; the All the Things You Are quarter-note triplet remains explicit.

Cross-check: matching refrain notation in neonscribe/lilypond-lead-sheets at commit a795f409963c48cb1a33cd0171987830cb4594b9. Differences were resolved against the supplied screenshots: Autumn Leaves' tied tonic into bar 16, syncopated approach in bar 24, descending chromatic note in bar 25 and final pickup; All the Things You Are holds the final tonic into bar 36. The uploaded full PDF exceeded the transfer limit and was not used for extraction. No melody was fetched from the network-blocked effendi.me site.

Playback uses the current style's existing melodic instrument and mixer channel. Written pitches transpose with the chart key. Activity settings and the arranger's later-chorus variations do not remove or reharmonise melody notes. Supporting/Countermelody/Featured is disabled while a written melody is active; its existing help line names the tune. The other backing parts retain their current arrangement. The chord charts retain their previous practice versions and extensions; they are not replaced by the screenshot's chord versions.

A short musical pickup precedes the first downbeat when Melodic movement is audible and count-in is off. A requested count-in includes the pickup in its final beats. Muted melody adds no automatic lead-in. Backed notation exercises keep their existing start clock; their pickups require the existing count-in option. Printed end-of-form pickups remain within the head. No extra pickup is inserted over a written final sustained note.

Straight styles retain printed subdivisions; swing styles swing written eighths for the swing tunes. Blue Bossa retains its straight rhythm; written triplets remain triplets. Loops follow the original chart beat positions and resume held notes at a loop entry. Exercise playback carries those original positions into its private chart and leaves the added resolution bar clear. Repeated whole-form Study passes repeat the head. A changed metre or incompatible form length pauses the written melody and explains this in the existing sound-settings help line.

Validation covers all four full-form event sequences, source keys, chromatic notes, tied durations, triplets, transposition, activity independence, loop entry, count-in/pickup clocks, muting, exercise resolution, later repeats and render-block independence. Browser checks cover standard loading, saved identity, the existing mixer, the pickup display and Study/exercise playback.

Pickup clock correction: count-in/pickup beats are separate from the song beat counter. The first downbeat emits song beat zero and bar-one beat one; chart highlighting consumes that song clock directly. Metronome accents and subdivision accents use the same song-relative beat. Transport initialization uses the same lead-in calculation as the engine, preventing a premature first-chord highlight.
