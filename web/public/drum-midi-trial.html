What was preserved, and what was adapted?
  • The MIDI versions retain the drummer’s note timing and note velocities. No quantization or extra MIDI humanization was applied.
  • The current version uses the app’s normal arrangement and human feel. Each style uses the same sampled kit for all three versions, without reverb or delay sends.
  • “MIDI groove” plays an excerpt starting after two introductory bars. “MIDI + fill” replaces the final bar with a separately performed fill from the same drummer/session and tempo. These joins are edits for this trial.
  • The app has fewer articulations than the recorded electronic kit: middle/floor toms share a low-tom sound, and continuous hi-hat openness is approximated by closed/open/pedal note types.
  • Levels are matched with one fixed gain per clip. No compression is added. There is a short natural decay after the resolving beat.

Use headphones if possible. A MIDI performance and the sound used to play it are separate choices; these clips let you judge the performance with our existing sounds.
